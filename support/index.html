Hapus akunDelete your account

Di app: buka tab Profil, ketuk Hapus akun, lalu konfirmasi. Akunmu, foto yang kamu ambil, dan data yang hanya milikmu langsung dihapus. Kalau Serumah-mu masih punya anggota lain, peralatan dan jadwal rumah tetap untuk mereka, dan perawatan yang kamu selesaikan tetap tercatat tanpa namamu. In the app: open the Profile tab, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account, the photos you took and the data that is only yours are deleted right away. If your household has other members, the home's appliances and schedule stay with them, and the cleanings you finished stay on record without your name.

Tidak bisa membuka app? Kirim email dari alamat akunmu ke domura.official@gmail.com dengan judul "Hapus akun". Kami menghapusnya dan membalas emailmu. Can't open the app? Email domura.official@gmail.com from your account's address with the subject "Delete account". We delete it and reply to you.

Gabung ke SerumahJoin a household

Ketuk link undangan dari anggota rumahmu, atau masukkan kodenya di tab Serumah. Setiap undangan berlaku 24 jam untuk satu orang. Tap the invite link from your household member, or enter its code in the Household tab. Each invite is valid for 24 hours, for one person.
